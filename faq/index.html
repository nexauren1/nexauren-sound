QUESTIONS

Answers before you press download.

A few common questions about the store and its digital products.

What do I get when I buy a product?

You receive the digital files described on the product page. The exact formats and contents will be listed with each product.

Are the products royalty-free?

Licensing depends on the product. The applicable license will be provided on the product page or with the download before the store is fully live for paid delivery.

Can I find free products?

Yes. Use the Free Sounds page to see products marked as free.

What if I need help?

Use the Contact page for store questions or issues with a product once customer support is connected.
